One important caveat

Don't optimize around a single “AI visibility score.” AI answers are nondeterministic: the same prompt can produce different citations and rankings at different times. Research published in 2026 found substantial variability in citation visibility and argues that these metrics should be treated statistically rather than as precise fixed numbers.
arXiv

I'd therefore prioritize tools that expose:

Exact prompts being monitored

Brand vs. competitor mentions

Citation/source URLs

Share of voice / position

Which prompts you're losing

Which sources cause competitors to be recommended

Tracking across ChatGPT + Perplexity + Gemini + Google AI Overviews, rather than one model

That prompt/source-level view is also what practitioners identify as the biggest differentiator between useful monitoring and a pretty dashboard.
